(Studio)Brainwashed®

We build lasting careers for artists who move culture forward.

Brainwashed started as an underground music zine in September 1996. It grew into an entertainment and culture company rooted in Indonesia's independent music scene.

Our work combines honest strategy, day-to-day representation, live development, and an instinct for what makes an artist impossible to ignore.
