01 / About

Not manufactured.
Not temporary.

Brainwashed began as an independent zine in September 1996. A year later, it evolved into Brainwashed Entertainment and Brainwashed Management—working with artists, ideas, and movements that shape Indonesia’s alternative culture.

Rooted in Jakarta’s independent music scene, Brainwashed brings decades of hands-on experience in artist management, live entertainment, media, and creative development. We build enduring careers through clear strategy, trusted relationships, and an instinct for culture.
